Guinness Chili

Ingredients

  1. 1 1/2 pounds lean ground beef
  2. 1 1/2 cups chopped onion
  3. 2 tablespoons chili powder
  4. 4 cloves garlic, minced
  5. 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
  6. 3/4 teaspoon salt
  7. 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  8. 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  9. 1 (28 ounce) can fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  10. 2 (15 ounce) cans kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  11. 1 (11.2 ounce) bottle stout beer, such as Guinness®
  12. 1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce
  13. 2 tablespoons pure maple syrup
  14. 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  15. sour cream, for garnish
  16. chopped green onions (optional)

Directions

  • Step 1

    Cook ground beef and onion in a 6-quart pot over medium heat, breaking up beef with a wooden spoon, until beef is no longer pink, about 8 minutes. Drain grease.

  • Step 2

    Season with chili powder, garlic, cumin, salt, cayenne pepper, and cinnamon. Cook and stir until fragrant, 1 minute.

  • Step 3

    Mix in undrained tomatoes, beans, beer, Worcestershire, maple syrup, and tomato paste. Bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, uncovered, until thickened, 20 to 25 minutes.

  • Step 4

    Garnish with sour cream and green onions, if desired.
