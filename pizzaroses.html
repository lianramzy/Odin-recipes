Pizza Roses

Ingredients

  • cooking spray
  • 1 package crescent dough, such as Jus Rol
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 ounces shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 8 large slices pepperoni, cut into thirds (or as needed)
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons pesto

Directions

  1. Step 1

    Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). Lightly grease a muffin tin with cooking spray.

  2. Step 2

    Unroll the crescent dough onto a clean surface. If needed, gently press seams together to form one rectangle. Cut into long strips about 2 inches wide.

  3. Step 3

    Spread about 2 teaspoons pizza sauce evenly across each strip and sprinkle with mozzarella cheese. Place large pepperoni slices along one edge, allowing them to slightly overlap the top edge for a “rose” appearance

  4. Step 4

    Starting at one end, roll each strip into a spiral. Place each rose into the cavity of a muffin tin.

  5. Step 5

    Stir together melted butter and Cantanzaro herbs in a small bowl. Brush herb butter lightly over each roll and drizzle a small amount of pesto over the tops.

  6. Step 6

    Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown, 12 to 15 minutes. Remove from oven and allow to cool for 5 minutes before serving.
